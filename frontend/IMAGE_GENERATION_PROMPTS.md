## Image handling notes
- Keep product images square and centered with room around the object.
- Use wide 16:9 imagery for the hero.
- Generate clean backgrounds so images sit naturally on white or pale-lavender cards.
- Avoid invented logos, visible brand names, watermarking, excessive neon, or text baked into images.
- Reviews, discounts, prices, and stock remain sourced from application data; generated images should only supply visuals.
